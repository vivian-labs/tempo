## Remaining acceptance boundaries

- macOS CI launch is a smoke test, not visual, speaker/headphone, sleep/resume or Retina acceptance on a physical Mac.
- Continuous-clock behavior during sleep and manual wall-clock changes is covered by deterministic kernel tests; this session did not put the user's machine to sleep or change its clock.
- CPAL opened the real Windows output device. No laboratory end-to-end audio latency or jitter measurement was made.
- Daily alarms require the application to remain running; the app does not wake a sleeping computer.
- Development bundles are unsigned. macOS notarization and paid signing identities are not configured.
